# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

SARGAM event heads, Core Team members, and teacher in-charge staff coordinating daily festival work.

## Product Purpose

Record daily work, let authorised team members review progress, and manage role-based access during SARGAM 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React and Supabase application with role-based task logs, team management, and calendar-based daily entries. Existing functionality must remain intact.

## Brand Commitments

SARGAM 2026 is a national-level intercollegiate cultural fest with the Mahadevi theme. The supplied fire-red, antique-gold, and deep-charcoal poster and SARGAM wordmark are binding assets.

## Evidence on Hand

Poster reference supplied in the conversation. Wordmark asset: `src/assets/sargam-brown.png`.

## Product Principles

- Make daily work easy to scan and update.
- Preserve role boundaries in every workflow.
- Carry the festival identity without compromising operational clarity.
