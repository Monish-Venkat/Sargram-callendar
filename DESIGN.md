# SARGAM Mahadevi Design System

## Visual world

An operational command centre lit like the festival stage: near-black charcoal fields, ember-red activity states, antique-gold navigation, and restrained ceremonial texture.

## Foundations

- **Background:** deep wine-black with low-opacity ember radial lighting and a fine grain overlay.
- **Accent:** ember red for committed actions; antique gold for identity, current state, and approved work.
- **Type:** an editorial serif for major page titles; a high-legibility sans serif for all task controls and data.
- **Surfaces:** dark layered panels with thin gold-tinted rules, never generic white cards.

## Components

- The app bar carries the supplied SARGAM wordmark on a dark translucent stage.
- The sidebar reads as a backstage navigation rail with a red-to-gold active signal.
- Calendar cells are dark ledger tiles; work entries glow ember-red and approved entries turn gold.
- Forms and review tables retain clear contrast and familiar controls while inheriting the dark ceremonial material.

## Responsive behavior

The wordmark scales down in the compact header, the sidebar becomes a horizontal mobile section, and calendar tiles remain touch-sized.
